- **Content Generation** - Generate professional email copy instantly

**Ensure your emails reach the inbox**

- **IP Warmup System** - Intelligent warming for new sending IPs

- **Provider-Specific Optimization** - Tailored sending for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC Ready** - Full authentication support
- **Bounce & Complaint Handling** - Protect your sender reputation

**Data-driven decisions made easy**

- **Real-Time Dashboard** - Monitor campaigns as they happen
- **Open Rate Tracking** - See who's reading your emails

### 6. Multi-Domain Management

**Run multiple brands from one platform**

### 7. SMTP Relay Configuration

**Flexible sending infrastructure**

- **Multiple SMTP Servers** - Configure backup and load-balanced sending
- **Rotation & Load Balancing** - Distribute sending across servers
- **Health Monitoring** - Automatic detection of server issues
- **Custom Ports & Encryption** - TLS/SSL support for secure sending
